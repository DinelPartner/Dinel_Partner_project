Many houses and older apartments in the Stockholm area have electrical panels that are 30–40 years old. If your fuses trip often, if the panel still uses screw-in fuses instead of circuit breakers, or if it lacks a residual-current device (RCD) entirely, these are clear signs that an upgrade is needed.

A modern electrical panel gives you better protection against electrical fires and accidents, makes troubleshooting easier, and creates room for future needs such as an EV charger, a heat pump, or additional circuits in the house. It's also often required by insurance companies and a natural part of any renovation or change of ownership.

The replacement is normally done in a single day by a licensed electrician, and we always review which circuits are needed before starting the work so you avoid surprises. Get in touch and we'll take a look at your current panel and give you a clear, concrete answer on whether — and when — it's time to replace it.
