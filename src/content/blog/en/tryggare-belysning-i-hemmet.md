Good lighting isn't just about atmosphere — it's about safety. Poorly lit stairs, entrances and driveways are a common cause of falls, especially during the dark Swedish winter months. Motion-activated outdoor lighting at the entrance is a simple improvement that increases safety and saves energy at the same time.

Indoors, it's worth reviewing the lighting in stairwells and hallways, where sharp contrasts between dark and bright surfaces can make it hard to judge distances. Dimmable LED fixtures let you adjust the light to the time of day, which is both easier on the eyes and uses less electricity.

Don't forget the electrical safety of your lighting either: old lamp holders, damaged cables or circuits that are too weak can be a fire risk.
